The thing I hate most: talking about myself.

I'm Kenyan, currently still residing in my lovely country, blessed with some rather questionable — let's blur that word — leaders.

I'm probably one of those dudes you'd expect to be doing this sort of thing, even though I don't really talk much about what I do. I just suddenly get the urge to build something, so I swing on that rope until I slide.

Most of what you see on this website comes from things I've worked on, learned, broken, fixed, and occasionally broken again.

This website is almost **99% vibecoded** because, honestly, I know very little about web development. I'm learning as I go, though, and I'm slowly figuring out how all of this works.

For now, I'm maintaining it myself and turning it into my own little corner of the internet. I'll eventually find somewhere to host it properly, but for the moment, localhost is home.
